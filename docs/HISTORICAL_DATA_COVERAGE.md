The report separates source, category, cadence, raw chart name, first/last observed date, row counts, distinct dates, identifiable track/artist/album counts, stream availability, and source permission state. Years 2017–2026 without staged rows are explicitly labeled `NO_VALIDATED_OBSERVATIONS`; that means no validated rows were staged, not that the source contains no data.

## Interpretation limits

- `US`, `USA`, `United States`, and `United States of America` are the only normalized row-level US values.
- Unknown, global, and other-country rows are not included in US staged observations.
- A missing date is a candidate gap only. The expected chart publication schedule has not been independently verified.
- Missing chart observations are not zero-stream observations.
- Calendar-day coverage is labeled diagnostic and must not be interpreted as chart completeness.
- Source overlap uses a stable track ID plus category/cadence/chart/date; conflicting positions, streams, artist credits, or titles are retained as conflicts, not summed.
- An empty coverage report is not proof of data absence on Kaggle; it can also reflect a failed or unavailable authenticated download.
- The values `popularity`, followers, monthly listeners, rank, and chart score are never treated as streams.

Until local source rows, USA semantics, the reporting calendar, data provenance, and rights are verified, no complete 2017–2026 period is claimed.
