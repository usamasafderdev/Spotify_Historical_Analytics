# Historical Data Gaps

## Client reporting window

The requested period is USA chart observations for 2017–2026. The application shows all years in this interval, including years without imported observations. No-observation labels do not mean zero streams or zero chart activity.

Research observations are not imported or exposed by the production application. A candidate missing date is only a date absent between observed dates; without a validated expected chart schedule it does not prove an archive gap.

## What closes a gap

For every required chart and year, obtain a source with documented provenance, explicit reporting cadence and market, valid dates and stable identities, documented stream semantics, correction/deletion history, and written authorization covering acquisition, analytics, commercial use, and planned exports/redistribution. Then validate coverage against an independently verified expected schedule and disclose any remaining gaps.
